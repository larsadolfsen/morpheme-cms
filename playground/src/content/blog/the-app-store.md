An app gives your site something it couldn’t do before: new blocks, new kinds of pop-ups, new settings or something on every page.

## Find it in the editor

Open Apps in the editor to browse the store. Each app has its own page with screenshots, a description, the cookies it sets and the addresses it loads from. Install it, and it’s added to your site in a single change.

## Updates you can trust

You always see what’s new before an app updates. Fixes and new features arrive automatically, while bigger changes wait until you tap Update.

Read more on the [App store](/app-store/) page.
