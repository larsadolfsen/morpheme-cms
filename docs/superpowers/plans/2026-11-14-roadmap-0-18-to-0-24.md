# Roadmap 0.18 to 0.26

> **Status:** approved by the owner on 14 November 2026. Written against `main` at `646ace0` (`@morphemecms/cms`
> 0.16.0, with 0.17.0 in the Release PR #340). Updated the same day with the owner's later decisions on 0.18, 0.19 and
> 0.20. On 17 November 2026 the owner added a new **0.19 Cards and lists**: the old 0.19 and every later line
> moved up by one. Documents written before 17 November use the old numbers: add one from 0.19 on.

**The owner's rule:** each version is one area of the app, plus the bug fixes and small fixes of that area.

Each item names the spec or plan it comes from (in `docs/superpowers/`) and its status:

- **spec ready**: it can be built.
- **owner questions open**: the spec waits for the owner's answers.
- **spec being written**: the owner has decided; a session writes the spec.
- **needs a design**: there is no spec yet.

## 0.18 Editor: writing a page

Writing and arranging a page in the editor.

- **Images and block flow** (intuitive editor batch 15): alt text right after an image is picked, the `Hide on the page`
  wording, closing a sheet. `specs/2026-11-11-intuitive-editor-design.md`. Spec ready.
- **Image tools** (owner decisions, 14 November). Spec being written:
  `specs/2026-11-14-image-tools-design.md`.
  - **No resize.** Images stay full width; there is no "drawn size". An image at another width is another block.
    Replaces the open questions of `specs/2026-11-13-resize-image-design.md`.
  - **A crop view:** full screen, the image in its real proportions, `Format` renamed `Aspect ratio`, the area outside
    the frame darkened with a border, zoom and move.
  - **The image tools in the one bar:** Change image, Crop, Alt text, Remove; for a video, Change address. Handed over
    from 0.17.
  - **One aspect ratio per Cards block**, for all its cards.
- **Command palette and one action registry** (owner decisions, 14 November).
  `specs/2026-11-13-command-palette-and-context-menus-design.md`.
  - It searches actions, the menu's places, and pages and posts by title. Files come later.
  - It opens with `Ctrl+K` and a plain search icon in today's top bar. No FAB.
  - Text actions on a phone too. No owner question open.
  - **One icon per meaning** (section 3.1): `tune` is the settings of the thing you are on (block, section, card, image,
    list); `settings` (the gear) is the editor's Settings and an app's Settings tab only; `edit` (the pencil) edits
    content somewhere else.
- **Block action row fixes:** inside the dotted outline, the Card shape, the theme's buttons. Merged (#640).
- **A choice group replaces segmented buttons** (owner decisions, 14 November): a connected button group when the
  choices fit on one line, otherwise a list of radio buttons, with a live sample where a choice is a look.
  `specs/2026-11-14-choice-group-design.md`. Spec ready.
- **One place to save while writing** (owner decisions, 14 November): the block's side sheet saves with the page;
  `Publish` moves to the page's app bar; the draft saves itself. On a phone the full-screen sheet keeps `Save` and
  `Cancel`. `specs/2026-11-14-one-save-while-writing-design.md`. Spec ready.
- **Only Content open** when a page or post opens. Merged (#643).
- **No top image on pages** (owner decisions, 14 November): pages lose the main heading's background image; posts keep
  theirs. No migration. `specs/2026-11-14-no-page-top-image-design.md`. Merged (#658).
- **Standard names** (owner, 14 November): use the standard name of a field or feature (Material 3's, the web's, the
  real product's), never a new one. `specs/2026-11-14-standard-names-audit.md`. An audit and a plan; its renames are
  built in 0.18.
- **Paste several lines to make items, and turn one block into another** (owner decisions, 14 November). Spec being
  written: `specs/2026-11-14-paste-and-change-to-design.md`.
  - Pasted lines become items in every repeating block: List, Steps, Segmented list, FAQ, Cards, Table.
  - `Change to…` turns one of those blocks into another and keeps the fields the new block hides.
- **Open editor bugs**, and the editor rows of `specs/2026-11-13-editor-uses-theme-parts-audit.md`. Spec ready.
- **Image grid follow-ups:** a separate SEO alt-text PR, a Preview check of the Library hint in the alt sheet, a manual
  `check:contrast`, lightbox motion. Spec ready.
- **Nightly CMS update:** a site checks npm for a new CMS once a day, at night in the site's time zone. Merged (#637).
- **Saved for the next version** (`todo-next-version.md`): the CMS version app on staging gets English texts (repo
  `morpheme-staging`). Spec ready.

## 0.21 Screens: app bars, sheets and autosave

Every screen gets M3's app bars, and settings screens become sheets that save the way a page does.

- **App bars on every screen** (owner decisions, 14 November; read from m3.material.io/components/app-bars the same
  day). Owner questions open (proposals P1–P8): `specs/2026-11-14-app-bars-design.md`.
  - **Search app bar** on the start screen and the list screens ("Search pages"). The search field moves up from
    today's search row. The sort, filter and date buttons become a row of chips under the bar.
  - **Pages and Posts open with the search bar shown.**
  - **Small app bar** on the page being written: back, the title, the search icon and Publish. No overflow menu in the
    app bar; the writing tools stay in the toolbar.
  - **Medium flexible app bar** on detail screens and the side sheet's header.
  - The search icon of 0.18 becomes the search app bar here.
  - The search-row rule in `morpheme-website`'s `CLAUDE.md` (*Søgeknapper*) changes with it.

- **Settings pages open as a subpage or a side sheet** (owner decisions, 14 November; full-screen sheets are not Material 3
  and bottom sheets are for menus and supplementary content):
  - On a phone (compact), a **subpage with a back arrow** (app bar: back arrow and headline only), and **Save** in the bottom bar the page form uses. No autosave.
  - On medium and expanded widths, a **side sheet** next to the page, with **Save** and **Cancel**. No autosave.
  - Leaving with unsaved changes (back arrow, browser back, Cancel) asks with the existing "Keep your changes?" dialog.
  - The same pattern is used for a file in Files (0.18).
  - While the owner types, a hidden copy is kept in the browser on that device. It is offered back ("Restore your unsaved
    changes?") only if the form closed without Save or Cancel. Save or Cancel deletes it.
  - Owner questions open (O1 which forms, O2 standard or modal side sheet; proposal P1): `specs/2026-11-14-settings-sheets-design.md`.
- **Autosave everywhere, phase 2.** `specs/2026-11-14-autosave-everywhere-design.md`. Decisions 1 (waiting versions in
  R2 only, never git) and 7 (Remove field and Remove data wait for the final Save) are agreed. Owner questions open (2–6
  and 8–10). Its scope is redrawn around the sheets (its section 0 and build plan 5a, 14 November); question 11 added.
- **Dashboard leftovers**, if the owner wants them. `specs/2026-11-07-dashboard-widgets-design.md` (agreed
  7 November). Spec ready.

## 0.26 Store: paid themes and apps

Selling in the store: its own release, after App platform and Platform (owner, 15 November 2026). It builds on both:
storage for apps decides what a pause keeps, Platform's accounts are who pays, and its GitHub App per site makes the
pause and resume commits.

- **Paid themes and apps.** Monthly and yearly subscriptions through Stripe (Checkout, Billing, Tax, Customer Portal);
  a lapsed item pauses on the site after a grace period and nothing is deleted; an inactive app's lists show as inactive
  under *Lists* with a way back. `morpheme-appstore` `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-paid-items-design.md` and
  `plans/2026-11-15-paid-items.md`. Owner questions answered; the spec is revised against 0.24 and 0.25 first. Needs the store auto-updates core first.

## How the lines run

- `0.18` was cut from `main`. Each later line's branch is cut from the previous line's branch when its work starts:
  `0.19` was cut from `0.18` on 14 Nov 2026, before 0.19 became Cards and lists (owner question open: does that work
  go on it).
- `0.18` is merged into `0.19` after each merge into `0.18`. `0.19` is never merged into `0.18` or `main` until 0.17 and
  0.18 are released and the owner says go.
- While 0.17 is unreleased, `main` is merged into `0.18` after each merge into `main`.
- After each release, `main` is merged into the next line.
- The levels each branch allows: skill `changesets`.
