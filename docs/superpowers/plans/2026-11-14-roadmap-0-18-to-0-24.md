# Roadmap 0.18 to 0.24

> **Status:** approved by the owner on 14 November 2026. Written against `main` at `646ace0` (`@morphemecms/cms`
> 0.16.0, with 0.17.0 in the Release PR #340). Updated the same day with the owner's later decisions on 0.18, 0.19 and
> 0.20.

**The owner's rule:** each version is one area of the app, plus the bug fixes and small fixes of that area.

Each item names the spec or plan it comes from (in `docs/superpowers/`) and its status:

- **spec ready**: it can be built.
- **owner questions open**: the spec waits for the owner's answers.
- **spec being written**: the owner has decided; a session writes the spec.
- **needs a design**: there is no spec yet.

## 0.18 Editor: writing a page

Writing and arranging a page in the editor.

- **Images and block flow** (intuitive editor batch 15): alt text right after an image is picked, the `Hide on the page`
  wording, closing a sheet. `specs/2026-11-11-intuitive-editor-design.md`. Spec ready.
- **Image tools** (owner decisions, 14 November). Spec being written:
  `specs/2026-11-14-image-tools-design.md`.
  - **No resize.** Images stay full width; there is no "drawn size". An image at another width is another block.
    Replaces the open questions of `specs/2026-11-13-resize-image-design.md`.
  - **A crop view:** full screen, the image in its real proportions, `Format` renamed `Aspect ratio`, the area outside
    the frame darkened with a border, zoom and move.
  - **The image tools in the one bar:** Change image, Crop, Alt text, Remove; for a video, Change address. Handed over
    from 0.17.
  - **One aspect ratio per Cards block**, for all its cards.
- **Command palette and one action registry** (owner decisions, 14 November).
  `specs/2026-11-13-command-palette-and-context-menus-design.md`.
  - It searches actions, the menu's places, and pages and posts by title. Files come later.
  - It opens with `Ctrl+K` and a plain search icon in today's top bar. No FAB.
  - Text actions on a phone too; one icon per meaning (`tune`, `settings`, `edit`). No owner question open.
- **Paste several lines to make items, and turn one block into another** (owner decisions, 14 November). Spec being
  written: `specs/2026-11-14-paste-and-change-to-design.md`.
  - Pasted lines become items in every repeating block: List, Steps, Segmented list, FAQ, Cards, Table.
  - `Change to…` turns one of those blocks into another and keeps the fields the new block hides.
- **Open editor bugs**, and the editor rows of `specs/2026-11-13-editor-uses-theme-parts-audit.md`. Spec ready.
- **Image grid follow-ups:** a separate SEO alt-text PR, a Preview check of the Library hint in the alt sheet, a manual
  `check:contrast`, lightbox motion. Spec ready.
- **Saved for the next version** (`todo-next-version.md`): sites update the CMS once a day at night; the CMS version app
  on staging gets English texts (repo `morpheme-staging`). Spec ready.

## 0.20 Screens: app bars, sheets and autosave

Every screen gets M3's app bars, and settings screens become sheets that save the way a page does.

- **App bars on every screen** (owner decisions, 14 November; read from m3.material.io/components/app-bars the same
  day). Needs a design (skill `material-3`).
  - **Search app bar** on the start screen and the list screens ("Search pages"). The search field moves up from
    today's search row. The sort, filter and date buttons become a row of chips under the bar.
  - **Pages and Posts open with the search bar shown.**
  - **Small app bar** on the page being written: back, the title, the search icon and Publish. No overflow menu in the
    app bar; the writing tools stay in the toolbar.
  - **Medium flexible app bar** on settings sheets and detail screens.
  - The search icon of 0.18 becomes the search app bar here.
  - The search-row rule in `morpheme-website`'s `CLAUDE.md` (*Søgeknapper*) changes with it.

- **Settings screens become sheets** (owner decisions, 14 November):
  - On large screens, a side sheet slides over the page, and the page keeps its space. The side sheet belongs to the page
    and follows the page's autosave.
  - On a phone, a full-screen sheet with Save and Cancel and **no autosave** (autosave there is counterintuitive).
  - While the owner types on a phone, a hidden copy is kept in the browser on that device. It is offered back ("Restore
    your unsaved changes?") only if the sheet closed without Save or Cancel. Save or Cancel deletes it.
  - Needs a design: a spec with the real M3 pages (full-screen dialog, side sheet, bottom sheet; skill `material-3`).
- **Autosave everywhere, phase 2.** `specs/2026-11-14-autosave-everywhere-design.md`. Decisions 1 (waiting versions in
  R2 only, never git) and 7 (Remove field and Remove data wait for the final Save) are agreed. Owner questions open (2–6
  and 8–10). Its scope must be redrawn around the sheets.
- **Dashboard leftovers**, if the owner wants them. `specs/2026-11-07-dashboard-widgets-design.md` (agreed
  7 November). Spec ready.

## How the lines run

- `0.18` was cut from `main`. Each later line's branch is cut from the previous line's branch when its work starts:
  `0.19` was cut from `0.18` on 14 Nov 2026.
- `0.18` is merged into `0.19` after each merge into `0.18`. `0.19` is never merged into `0.18` or `main` until 0.17 and
  0.18 are released and the owner says go.
- While 0.17 is unreleased, `main` is merged into `0.18` after each merge into `main`.
- After each release, `main` is merged into the next line.
- The levels each branch allows: skill `changesets`.
