# Screens of the page form, before and after (batch 12, step 4a)

375 px wide, the demo's page "About Morpheme".

- `before-published-edited.png`, `after-published-edited.png`: a published page with the title changed. Before: the `Status` menu in
  Page settings and `Save changes`. After: no `Status` menu; `Save changes` keeps the changes as a waiting version and
  `Publish changes` puts them on the website.
- `before-published-bottom.png`, `after-published-bottom.png`: the end of the form. After: `Unpublish` below the sections.
- `after-draft.png`: a draft page: the line under the title says `Draft` and the bar has `Publish`.
- `after-waiting.png`, `after-waiting-bottom.png`: a published page with a waiting version: `Unpublished changes` under the title,
  `Publish changes`, and `Unpublish` and `Discard changes` at the end of the form.

Step 4b: `after-schedule-dialog.png` (the `Schedule` dialog), `after-scheduled.png` (`Scheduled · <time>` under the title) and
`after-scheduled-bottom.png` (`Schedule`, `Cancel schedule`, `Unpublish` and `Discard changes` at the end of the form).

Step 4c-2: `after-switch-changes.png` (a page with changes waiting: the `Live` / `Changes` switch at the top, the changes being edited) and
`after-switch-live.png` (the live page being edited: `Editing the live version`, no `Publish changes`). Before, there was no switch: the
form always showed the waiting changes, and the live page could not be edited while they waited.
