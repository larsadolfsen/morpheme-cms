# Screens of a new page with autosave

375 px wide, the demo's new page form after typing the H1 "About our team".

- `before-new-page.png`: before, the line under the title says `Not saved yet` until `Save changes`.
- `after-saving.png`, `after-saved.png`: with Faster editor on, the line says `Saving…` and then `Saved`.
- `after-faster-editor-off.png`: with Faster editor off, it says `Not saved` until `Save changes`, and the page is made on `Save changes` as before.
