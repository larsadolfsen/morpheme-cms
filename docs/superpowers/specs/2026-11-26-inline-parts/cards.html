The Cards block in the page editor, with the same rules

Cards are drawn as the site draws them. Only the active card shows its parts: the image (tap to change it), headline, subhead and supporting text, each with a border and 48px high, and, at the bottom, Add button and Add link as outlined buttons with an icon. The active part's border turns blue; the solid ring is only for keyboard focus. The active card has its own menu (move, Add item, remove) and the block keeps its own menu; the toolbar holds only the page and its text. Empty parts ask for an action, with M3's names: Your headline, Your subhead, Your supporting text.
