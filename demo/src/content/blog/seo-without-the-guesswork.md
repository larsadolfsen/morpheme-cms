A great page is only great if people can find it. The editor helps while you write.

## A score on every page

Each page and post has an SEO score: is the title the right length, does the heading use your keyword, does every photo have a description?

## Internal links, automatically

Give a page a keyword, and that word becomes a link to the page wherever it appears across your site. You decide how many links a page can have.

## Redirects that stay tidy

When a page moves, its old address sends visitors to the new one, and the editor tells you where. Redirects always point straight to the final page, so they never form a chain.
