# Carousel

Drawn by the Carousel component: the Carousel block, in the layouts Material 3 names. Items are images or videos in every layout; in the two uncontained layouts an item can also be a card.

[All blocks](/blocks/)

## Carousel of videos

An item can be a video in every layout; it keeps its own play button. In the multi-aspect ratio layout each video is as wide as its own video is: 16:9 unless the item says another, so a landscape and a portrait video stand in one row. A video item can have a thumbnail of its own, as the Video block can: the last one does.
