The theme's Card follows Material 3's anatomy: it draws `media` or an `icon`, a `headline`, a `subhead`, `supporting` text and `actions` in M3's order. Filled cards sit on the highest surface container, a dragged card gets its state layer, and an outlined card's edge turns `on-surface` with focus. The card's rules and tokens move to `theme/styles/ui/card.css`.
