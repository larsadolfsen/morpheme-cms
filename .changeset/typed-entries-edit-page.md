A list's items are typed where they stand on its edit page, for every list (your own, Testimonials and an app's): type a title or text, add above or below, move up or down, and remove with Undo. What you type is saved by itself. A list's fields no longer have a Shown switch, because each block picks the field it shows in each place; fields a list had switched off show again.
