The text bar has its own Divider button, and Divider is no longer in the Insert list. A Divider in the text can be selected like a block: tap it to add above or below, move or remove it. A card with buttons can have a divider above them. In the editor, only text sections have dotted lines, full width, and the section with the cursor shows its lines in the focus colour. The bar has a button to hide or show the helping lines.
